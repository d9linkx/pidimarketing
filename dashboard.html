How it works

We help you get seen by the algorithm, so you can reach a wider audience faster.

01. Detail the task

Select your platform (Instagram, Google, etc.). Define your engagement goals and the specific instructions our performers must follow to ensure brand alignment.

02. Performers find you organically

We don't use links. You provide search words and visuals you can recognize e.g flyer, screenshot of the post, thumbnail, etc. This helps performers to find your content organically, signaling high quality to algorithms of these platforms.

03. Make the payment (refundable)

Funds are paid upon creating in a campaign. You're paying for verified, high-quality human engagements that meets the organic search criteria set by the algorithms of these platforms.

04. Review what was done

Track interactions in real-time. Access detailed analytics showing the retention impact and algorithm breakthrough achieved by your campaign missions.

01. Accept task

Browse the board and claim a mission before the pool fills up. Once accepted, you have 30 minutes to complete the task and submit proof before the spot is released. Failure to complete the task within the timeframe releases the task for someone else to claim and complete.

02. Start the task

Find the post organically using the Search words and visuals in the task description. You must stay on the content for at least 60 seconds before engaging to prove genuine human attention.

03. Use the '10-Word' rule (for comments)

Every comment must be at least 10 words long and contextually relevant. No "Cool!" or "Nice!". We only reward high-value human interactions.

04. Confirm you've done it

Upload a screenshot and paste your text. Our admins verify submissions within 24 hours. Rewards are credited to your balance upon approval.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is PIDItasks?
PIDItasks is a platform connecting Creators (brands/influencers) who need genuine social engagement with Performers (real people) who earn money by providing that engagement.
How does PIDItasks ensure organic reach for Creators?
We use a unique "Organic Discovery" method. Creators provide a Search Seed (e.g., @Username) and a Visual Fingerprint. Performers then find the content organically within native apps, mimicking genuine user behavior, which algorithms favor.
What is the "Rule of 10 Words"?
Every engagement (e.g., comment) submitted by a Performer must be at least 10 words long and contextually relevant. This ensures high-quality, human-like interactions, avoiding generic or bot-like responses.
How does the 30-minute task timer work for Performers?
When a Performer claims a task, a 30-minute timer begins. They must complete the task and submit proof within this timeframe. If the timer expires, the task is released back to the pool for other Performers.
What is the 60-second Dwell Factor?
After a Performer finds a Creator's content organically, they must remain on that content for at least 60 seconds (the "Dwell Phase") before engaging. This signals genuine attention to social algorithms and is tracked by our system.
How are Creator funds protected?
Creator funds are held in Escrow upon campaign launch. Payment is only released to Performers after their work is verified by our system and meets all quality criteria.
What kind of proof do Performers need to submit?
Performers must submit a screenshot of their engagement and paste the exact text of their comment (if applicable) for verification.
How long does it take for Performer submissions to be verified?
Our admin team typically verifies submissions within 24 hours. Once approved, rewards are credited to the Performer's balance.
Can Creators specify exact comments or only sentiment?
Creators provide "Sentiment Direction" or "Required Keywords/Context." Performers then craft unique, relevant comments that adhere to the "Rule of 10 Words," ensuring authenticity.
What happens if a Performer's submission is rejected?
If a submission is rejected (e.g., for not meeting the 10-word rule or unclear proof), the Performer will receive feedback. The task spot is then released for another Performer to claim.
What platforms does PIDItasks support?
PIDItasks supports major social media platforms like Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X (Twitter), LinkedIn, and search engines like Google and YouTube.
Is there a limit to how many tasks a Performer can do?
No, Performers can complete as many available tasks as they wish, provided they meet the quality standards and time limits for each.
How do Creators track their campaign progress?
Creators have access to a real-time dashboard showing campaign progress, engagement counts, and detailed analytics on the impact of their missions.
How do Performers get paid?
Rewards for approved tasks are credited to the Performer's in-app balance. They can then withdraw their earnings to their linked bank account or digital wallet.
What is a "Search Seed" and "Visual Fingerprint"?
A Search Seed is a keyword or username Performers use to find content (e.g., "best marketing agency" or "@brandname"). A Visual Fingerprint is a unique image or thumbnail that helps Performers identify the exact post.
Can Creators pause or cancel a campaign?
Creators can pause campaigns at any time. Funds for unfulfilled engagements can be refunded or reallocated to new campaigns. Funds for completed/verified tasks are non-refundable.
Is PIDItasks safe for my social media accounts?
Yes, safety is paramount. Our organic discovery and dwell time protocols mimic natural user behavior, making it indistinguishable from genuine engagement to platform algorithms. We avoid direct links, a common trigger for penalties.
What if I encounter a technical issue on the platform?
Our support desk is available to assist with any technical issues, login problems, or questions you may have. You can access it via the sidebar menu.
How does PIDItasks prevent bot activity?
We employ a multi-layered verification system including the "Rule of 10 Words," Dwell Factor tracking, and manual admin reviews to ensure all engagements come from real, attentive human Performers.
Can I use PIDItasks for personal or business accounts?
PIDItasks is designed for both personal brands (influencers, content creators) and businesses looking to boost their online presence and engagement metrics organically.

Refund Policy

Terms of Service & Refund Eligibility

At PIDI Marketing, we strive to ensure 100% satisfaction for every campaign launched. However, because we initiate real-time human engagement immediately upon payment verification, our refund policy is strictly structured to protect both our performers and our clients.

1. Non-Performance: If your campaign fails to reach the target quantity of engagements within the specified timeframe (standard 72 hours unless otherwise stated), you are eligible for a pro-rated refund for the unfulfilled portion of the campaign.

2. Quality Standards: Every engagement is verified by our AI "Bot-Killer" and manual audit team. If you find engagements that do not meet our "Rule of 10 Words" or quality guidelines, please report them within 24 hours for a credit replacement or partial refund.

3. Direct Payment: Please note that payments made via bank transfer are for specific service delivery. Once performers are assigned and tasks are claimed, the funds are committed to the workforce pool to ensure fair compensation for our human taskers.

4. Cancellation: Campaigns can be paused at any time, but funds already distributed to performers for completed tasks cannot be reversed. Remaining balances can be applied to new campaigns or refunded upon request.

5. Support: To request a review of your campaign, please contact the Support Desk with your Transaction ID. Our team typically resolves all claims within 48 hours. We value the trust you place in our network and ensure that every Naira spent contributes directly to your brand's growth through genuine human interaction. By proceeding with this transfer, you acknowledge that PIDI Marketing acts as a facilitator for verified attention and that results depend on the quality of the target content provided.
